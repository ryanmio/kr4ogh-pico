Not a KR4OGH flight. VE3VRO launched from grid EN94 in Ontario on 22 June
2026 and was still transmitting on 21 August, when this track was captured.
By then it had gone round the northern hemisphere close to three times.

It is here because it is what the decoder was checked against. One day of it,
18 August, matched the Traquito Flight Search Dashboard on all 67 records and
is frozen as this repository's test vectors; the full ingest above reproduces
those 67 records exactly. Details in
[`docs/decode-status.md`](https://github.com/ryanmio/kr4ogh-pico/blob/main/docs/decode-status.md).

Two caveats, since this is someone else's flight seen from public spot data:
there is no launch time on record, so the track starts at the first decoded
report, and none of the Pass/Fail scoring applies.
