This is not a KR4OGH flight. It is one full UTC day — **2026-08-18** — of
**VE3VRO**'s pico balloon on 20&nbsp;m channel&nbsp;343, mid-Pacific at the
time, ingested through this repository's own stack (wspr.live pull → SQLite →
fingerprint match → decode) while validating the decoder before our first
launch.

Every one of the 67 decoded records on this page was verified row-by-row
against the Traquito Flight Search Dashboard (67 of 67 identical on every
compared field) and spot-checked against the Traquito decode page (5 of 5
identical). The full comparison, including the rows that deliberately don't
appear here and why, is written up in
[`docs/decode-status.md`](https://github.com/ryanmio/kr4ogh-pico/blob/main/docs/decode-status.md),
and the raw spots behind it are frozen in the repository as test vectors.

It is archived here for two reasons: it exercises the site's static
flight-archive rendering end to end before a real flight needs it, and it is
a fair preview of what a day of Basic Telemetry actually looks like — 10-watt
microwatt-budget transmissions heard across an ocean, nightly silence
included.

Because this is a one-day sample of someone else's flight, there is no launch
point, no outcome classification, and no distance-flown claim. When F1A and
F1B close, their archives will replace this page as the interesting part of
this site.
